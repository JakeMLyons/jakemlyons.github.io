Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026.

This policy covers the Google API apps Jake Lyons runs for his own use, such as the one that copies his files into his own Google Drive. They are personal tools, not services: no one else signs in to them, and they hold no one else's data.

What they access

Only what the Google account owner grants at sign-in. The Drive app asks for one permission, to create files in the owner's Drive and to see the files it created itself. It cannot see or change any other file in that Drive.

How the data is used

  • Files are copied from the owner's own computers into the owner's own Drive, as backups, and read back only to restore them.
  • Nothing is shared with, sold to, or shown to anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising or to train any model.
  • The sign-in token is kept on the owner's own computer, readable only by the account that uses it, and is never sent anywhere but Google.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time from Google account permissions, and delete the copied files in Drive.
